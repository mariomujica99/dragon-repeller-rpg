# Dragon Repeller RPG

## Overview

This project is a text-based RPG adventure game where players must defeat a dragon. The player can navigate to different locations, purchase weapons, fight monsters, and level up the character. The game is won by defeating the dragon.

## Live Demo
[Play Now](https://mariomujica99.github.io/dragon-repeller-rpg/)

## Game Features

### Core Gameplay
- **Turn-Based Combat**: Fighting system with attack and dodge mechanics
- **Progression System**: Gain XP, collect gold, and upgrade weapons
- **Resource Management**: Balance health, gold, and inventory
- **Locations**: Town square, store, and cave environments
- **Hidden Easter Egg**: Secret mini-game with gambling mechanics

### Player Stats
- **XP (Experience Points)**: Increases with each monster defeated, affects damage output
- **Health**: Current health points (starts at 100)
- **Gold**: Currency for purchasing items (starts at 50)
- **Inventory**: Track owned weapons

### Combat System
- **3 Monster Types:**
  - **Slime** (Level 2, 15 HP) - Beginner enemy
  - **Fanged Beast** (Level 8, 60 HP) - Intermediate challenge
  - **Dragon** (Level 20, 300 HP) - Final boss

- **4 Weapons:**
  - **Stick** (5 power) - Starting weapon
  - **Dagger** (30 power) - 30 gold
  - **Claw Hammer** (50 power) - 30 gold
  - **Sword** (100 power) - 30 gold

### Game Mechanics
- **Damage**: Attack damage based on weapon power + random XP bonus
- **Monster**: Monster attack power scales with level
- **Weapon Durability**: 10% chance for weapons to break during combat
- **Health Recovery**: Purchase health at the store (10 gold for 10 HP)
- **Weapon Trading**: Sell weapons for 15 gold when inventory is full

### Winning Strategy
1. Start by fighting slimes to gain XP and gold
2. Purchase better weapons from the store
3. Fight fanged beasts for more XP and gold
4. Once strong enough, challenge the dragon
5. Defeat the dragon to win the game

### Easter Egg
- After defeating any type of monster, there's a secret option (far right button)
- Play a number guessing mini-game
- Win 20 gold or lose 10 health
